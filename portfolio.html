reciKEEP

A recipe-link organizer that keeps every recipe you find on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or the web in one place instead of scattered bookmarks. Paste a link and it reads the real title and photo automatically, then sorts recipes into custom categories and subcategories you control. Built with a Node/Express backend and Supabase for auth and data, with Google and email sign-in so your recipes follow you across devices.
